News Item of Mangalore program for PFCT students.

The annual program of MEET THE DONOR organised by GSB Scholarship League, Mumbai, for the beneficiaries of Prabhu Family Charitable Trust was held on the 12th of March at Sudheendra Hall of Canara High school , Mangalore.

Students residing in and around Mangalore were present by 10 morning, It was heartening to see the young enthusiastic students coming from, Mangalore, Udupi, Shimoga, Cochin, Karkala, Karwar, Kumta Kundapur, Dharwad etc. to meet their donor and participate in the interactive program.

This Program started with the melodious voice of Apoorva, with Ganapati Vandana, for an auspicious beginning. Smt.Gita R.Pai, Chairperson of the League. welcomed Sri. Ajit Prabhu and his daughter Anvita Ms.Prabhu, Sri.Suresh, Trustee of the PFCTrust, all beneficiary students, and their parents. The G S B Scholarship League and the Prabhu Family Charitable Trust bonded very well, because of the common mission to support the education of the Poor and needy students. It was heartening to see, in spite of all financial difficulties students have managed to go ahead with their education. 9 students of the final year graduation were called to give their feedback on getting financial assistance from PFCT and in what way it has helped them. Students spoke extremely well, expressed their gratitude to GSB Scholarship League and to PFCT.

Sri Ajit Prabhu, started giving his own life's example, and told the students, how to over some hardships that come in life, . Be on the lookout to grab opportunities. He said that his parents and teachers have supported his education and now it is his turn to give back to society. In the near future the present students to follow this example, so that coming generation will have better future. Anvita said that a better future needs a better environment and to strengthen this point she said Quest Global is distributing seeds for the students to plant supporting this green revolution.

Smt.Vijaya Kamath, Trustee of the League was in charge of the distribution of Scholarship Cheques to students. Sri Ganesh Shanbhag of GSB Scholarship and Sri Suresh of Prabhu Family Trust proposed vote of thanks, Program ended with a sumptuous lunch for all.
