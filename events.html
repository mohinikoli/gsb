18th January Program - MET THE DONOR



GSB Scholarship League held it s annual program of MEET THE DONOR in Mangalore at Raman Pai hall on Saturday the 18th of January.

Students who were told in advance about this program came to the Hall from all nearby places, some even came from Bangalore / Mysore/ Kochi.

On behalf of Prabhu Family Charitable Trust, the following members were present:

  • Mrs. Shalini Prabhu
  • Mrs. Ponam Prabhu
  • Mr. Avinash Prabhu
  • Mr. Raghavan R
  • Mr. Ravalnath Kamath
  • Mr. Suresh

The meeting started with Vaishnavi Pai, one of the students, singing a Ganapathi prayer.

Smt. Gita R. Pai welcomed all and guided students. She emphasized the importance of following the scholarship guidelines, attaching necessary documents, and adhering strictly to deadlines to ensure the continuity of the scholarship amount.

She also warned students about common mistakes that could lead to the loss of scholarship amounts.

She invited some final year students of 3 / 4 year degree students to talk and give their opinion on getting this Prabhu Charitable funds. Student’s reaction was worth listening. This has reduced their, worries of paying college fees and the applying for the bank loan for education.

Mr.Suresh, spoke to the students and gave them the incentive of offering internship / job at Quest Global. There was a loud applause from students. Mrs..Shalini Prabhu and Mr.Avinash Prabu, members of Prabhu family spoke on Student Scholrship family.

Sudha Prabhu on behalf of the League, proposed a vote of thanks, Special thanks were given to

  • Smt. Ujwala Mallya
  • Sri K. N. Bhat
  • Smt. Lata Kamath
  • Smt. Akshata Sudhir Pai

News item to be published on local papers, Student applications, GSB Scholarship League 2023-23

In view of the present prevailing conditions, GSB Scholarship League needs to communicate to its beneficiary students with regards to the Application forms for the academic year 2023-24 for the economically needy students, merit counts later.

Since the Pandemic 2020 , GSB Scholarship League has stopped giving hard copies of Application forms to the poor and needy G S B students who want to apply.

All students whose names and mobile numbers are registered with the League will be sent a link on their mobile numbers in the month of August 2023.

All instructions to get the application forms and to fill up the form is given. PLEASE read the instructions carefully, fill up the form and SUBMIT through ONLINE only.

Write your Bank details giving utmost importance to fill up correct details. All chosen students will get the scholarship through NEFT in their bank accounts. About a fortnights time after the last date, go through your bank statement and see that your scholarship amount has appeared in your Bank statement.

No bank cheques will be issued.

ONLINE applications are simple, applying is simple, minimum documents to attach.

In spite of this if you need to ask some more details, please send emails on info@gsbscholarshipleague.org All queries will be replied.

Last date for receiving application forms will be October 31st 2023. The server closes on that day. Late applications will be rejected by the server.

School going students in small villages, whose parents are not tech savvy need some service. College going students / seniors, can Help them to get application forms, and also fill up the application forms.

Funds permitting League might support some post graduate applicants.


Gita R.Pai
Chairperson
GSB Scholarship League

GSB Scholarship League's Program PILLARS OF STRENGTH


The GSB Scholarship League, oganised an Annual program which is called, PILLARS OF STRENGTH.

This program is to formally invite the Mumbai donors and thank them, get some of the opinion / suggestions from them regarding the Scholarship to students.

Mumbai's well known, Oncologist, Dr. Ramakant Deshpande was the chief guest.

President of the League, Sri S. S. Bhat sought the blessings of Almighty and welcomed the audience, Sri Ganesh Shanbhag, Secretary introduced the chief guest.

The Chairperson of the League, Smt. Gita R. Pai introduced the following 10 donors and requested them each to give their opinion on the League's workings / disbursement of funds, and fund collection. There were very good suggestions from the donors.

Sri G.M.Kamat carried League's report which was printed in 1919 and said that the 2 Annas given to the applicant student in that year was perhaps much more valuable that Rs.2,500 given for the students now. Gurudutt Gaitonde, Vasant Kamath and Neela Shenvi suggested that those supported students should show their gratitude by supporting the League once they start earning.

Smt. Anita Pai said there are many newer courses for newer careers, which should be supported. Dr. Sudhakar Shenai, appreciated the work being done to help the needy students. Sri Kukkunoor Madhav Kini narrated his personal experience as to how he has benefitted from the League and the pleasure he gets in donating to the League.

It was interesting to listen to all the speakers. Smt. Gita Pai aptly answered all the doubts the donors had regarding the collection of funds and disbursal of funds.

Dr. Ramakant Deshpande had a lot of inputs , based on the donors suggestions. Deshpande feels, community is very generous, and the education has also become very expensive. So collecting more money and supporting more needy students should be a good step forward. He appreciated the creators of this League and appreciated the idea of supporting the education of the NEEDY.

All the guests were honoured. Dr. Niranjani Nayak & Sri Raghuveer Nayak were thanked for sponsoring this program.

Secretary Sri Madhukar Pai proposed a hearty vote of thanks.

As in the past Wadala Mutt authorities were generous in supporting League's activities.

Special thanks were given to Sri Vinayak Shanbhag for sponsoring delicious snacks

Gita R. Pai
Chairperson

GSB Scholarship League, organised its Annual get together of Donors on 1st of May at Dwarkanath Bhavan Hall. Most of them were repeat donors . The Chief guest couple was Smt. & Sri Shashikant Shanbhag, and Industrialist and a philanthropist. Supporting the education of the needy was his main moto for which he supports the League

League's president, Sri S.S. Bhat welcomed the guest and the audience, Sri Ganesh Shanbhag, Secretary, introduced the chief guests. Smt. Gita R.Pai, Chairperson, of the League, elaborated and explained why it is necessary to hold such a program. Donors send money to the League generously and it is the duty of the Managing committee people to meet them in such programs and assure them that the funds are best utilised and lot of care is being taken to see that the funds go to the needy and poor students only.

Meet The Donor Program In Mangalore

GSB Scholarship League is over 112 years old with the sole mission to support the education of the Poor and NEEDY students. Prabhu Family Charitable Trust has the same ideas of helping the education of the needy. Since 2016 these two organisations have been jointly supporting many students, In the last 5 years 54 Engineering students have successfully completed their education and have got good placements. There was an investment of about Rs.26 Lakhs on these students. Smt. Gita R. Pai Chairperson of the GSB Scholarship League, gave these statistics, In her inaugural speech to the 70 odd PFTC students gathered in Canara College Hall during the PFTC’s MEET THE DONOR PROGRAM

Srinidhi sang Ganapati prayer to invoke the blessings of Lord. Ms. Poonam Prabhu, on behalf of the PFCT very charismatically told about the company Quest Global which Mr. Ajit Prabhu is heading. The students were amazed to know about Mr.Ajit Prabhu’s back ground which was simple and humble. She told, it is only good education and hard work that will lead to the desired destination of success. Mr. Niket Vice President of Quest Global in his brilliant speech told the students about discipline followed in the Navy, where he was earlier working, without wasting time and keeping the discipline and diligently working leads to the success. Mr.Suresh , Trustee, of PFCT requested the students to take an oath that they would support at least two students when they financially become independent, so that a day will come when the entire society will become fully educated.

Some final year students were asked to give feedback and their personal experience on getting PFCT support for all 4 years. How difficult it is to manage and how easily the 4 years of education were spent was the general opinion expressed by students. They were all full of praise to GSB Scholarship League and Prabhu Family Charitable Trust.

Smt.Gita Pai, Chairperson of the Scholarship League, thanked the Authorities of Canara High School, PFCT authorities, all the students present for making the program a wonderful one. All those present were asked to join for sumptuous lunch which was ready as students had to leave for their next destinations.
