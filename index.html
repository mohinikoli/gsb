Details on student application requirements

To all students who seek application forms for Scholarship for the year 2024-25

GSB Scholarship League’s server was not active till 1st of AUGUST 2024.

THE LAST DATE FOR SUBMITTING THE APPLICATION FORMS IS 31st OCTOBER 2024

Please send your request for application forms for Scholarship by giving the following details on info@gsbscholarshipleague.org

  1. Name of the student
  2. Surname
  3. ONE mobile number ONLY on which you want the message to get application form.
  4. Student’s Adhar Card number

Please DO NOT send any more details

After sending this email. Within 15 days ( or less ) you will get a message on your mobile number which gives you the TERMS & CONDITIONS and then a link get an Application form

Fill up all columns.

Upload the required documents, like, Marksheet, Fee receipt, Student Study certificate from the School / college & Adhar Card. (get current details ONLY)

Finally “SUBMIT” the application form and you get “THANK YOU”, which completes sending your form.

IF you have any doubt please call up / send an enquiry email on info@gsbscholarshipleague.org BEFORE SUBMITTING the form.

Once you submit the form. You will not get a second chance for correction.

Thank you for following the Instructions.

Smt. Gita R.Pai
Chairperson

Scholarship

Year after Year GSB Scholarship League has to express gratitude to Prabhu Family Charitable Trust, Specially to Mr. Ajit Prabhu and family for supporting the education of many graduate students. This Trust started supporting about 30 students and now the number is nearing 90. This has resulted in many good students completing their studies and have become Engineers, Doctors, Accountants and Chartered Accountant. GSB Scholarship League's Trustees and Managing Committee members are very grateful to this Trust and the Prabhu Family Members.

League History

In 1912, Gopal Subrao Dangi, Dr. Narayan Anna Naik, Madhavrao Vasudev Bhat, Sheshgiri Manjunath Kamat, Martobrao Durgappa Gaitonde, Vinayak Srinivas Kamat,Ramachandra Krishna Prabhu.

In July 1923 , the League formed a Trust and appointed a Board of Trustees to hold the funds of the League to ensure the safety and security of the property and investments of the League.

How Can You Help ?

New Endowment

An Endowment can be started with as little as 5,000/-! The Leagues's present annual disbursement of 1,800/- to a school student is funded by the income generated on an amount of close to 25,000/-.

Contribution

You are welcome to add any amount to your existing endowment at your discretion. All the funds received as endowments add to the Corpus.

Donation

Funds received as donations are disbursed to students during the same year.

Study Abroad

Loans are made to students who sek funds for their post-graduate education in foreign universities and are payable to the League with interest. Donation are collected in multiples of 50,00/-.
